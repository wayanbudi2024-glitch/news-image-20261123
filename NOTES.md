# Research notes — 23 November 2026 news image

Researched Monday 23 November 2026 (WITA) via news search and major outlets.
All five stories were the top news of Monday 23 Nov; where an event itself
happened on Sunday, it was first reported / confirmed on Monday and led
Monday's front pages. The image is dated Monday, 23 November 2026 and
labels nothing as happening on Monday that did not.

## Stories used

1. **COP31 collapses — no deal (Antalya, Turkey)**
   - The final plenary ended Sunday with no consensus; the collapse dominated
     Monday's front pages (BBC newspaper review, 23 Nov).
   - EU and UK rejected a draft with no mention of fossil fuels; first
     summit to fail to adopt an overarching deal since Copenhagen 2009.
   - Sources: CNN (22 Nov), Reuters (23 Nov analysis), The Times, The Hindu
     BusinessLine (published 23 Nov), Fox Business.

2. **Democrats take the House — 258 seats (United States)**
   - The 258th seat was clinched when Democrat Chuck Hubbard defeated Rep.
     Virginia Foxx in North Carolina's 5th district after a recount (330
     votes); AP called the race Sunday, and the milestone was reported as
     the lead US story on Monday 23 Nov.
   - Largest House majority since 1992; 44 seats flipped; four races still
     uncalled (Minnesota, Tennessee, two Louisiana runoffs).
   - Sources: Reuters, AP, Fox News, The Times, Washington Examiner.

3. **Russian warship sunk in Libya (Benghazi)**
   - Suspected Ukrainian sea drones struck the corvette Merkury at Benghazi
     naval base; the ship capsized and sank, ten people hurt. AP story
     published 23 Nov 2026; also a second commercial ship hit off Benghazi
     and a Russian cargo ship struck in the Baltic a day earlier.
   - Sources: AP (23 Nov), CNN, Wall Street Journal, Fox News, The Times.

4. **Saudi intercepts drone and missile (Middle East)**
   - Reported Monday 23 Nov: the Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted a
     Houthi drone bound for Riyadh and a ballistic missile aimed at the
     western Red Sea coast; Houthi media reported six wounded in a strike
     on a state-run oil company fuel station in Sanaa.
   - Context: Houthis had claimed strikes on the Rabigh refinery and a
     radar site on Sunday; Saudi authorities confirmed no refinery damage.
   - Sources: Reuters, Al Jazeera via KeNews (23 Nov), European Sun Times
     (23 Nov dateline).

5. **UK to host 2035 Women's World Cup (sport)**
   - FIFA confirmed in Zurich on Monday 23 Nov that England, Wales,
     Scotland and Northern Ireland will co-host the 2035 Women's World Cup
     — the first World Cup in the UK since 1966. (The US, Jamaica, Costa
     Rica and Mexico host in 2031.)
   - Sources: Reuters (22 Nov), BBC Sport, British Bulletin.

## Stories deliberately NOT used as main panels

- Two tankers struck in the Strait of Hormuz (Saturday 21 Nov) — too old.
- Trump friendship photos with Putin and Xi after the Shenzhen summit
  (22 Nov) — photo-op, less consequential.
- India vs New Zealand 1st Test, Day 5 (ongoing) — sport, lower weight
  than the FIFA confirmation.
- Mount Mayon eruption (Philippines) — date of the eruption could not be
  pinned to 23 Nov from the sources checked.
- Tyson Fury documentary / schizophrenia disclosure — entertainment, and
  the underlying health claim is a newspaper report, not a verified fact
  to present as news.
- French student protests — preparations reported 23 Nov, but the
  protests themselves were set for the coming weekend.
